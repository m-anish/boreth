A clock estate's real failure is not inaccuracy.
It is nobody noticing.

  • power cutThe clock does not stop, and is right the moment the lights return — or never goes dark at all, with the optional battery.
  • network downThe keeper runs on its own oscillator and marks every beacon as degraded.
  • keeper goneEvery dial keeps vigil on a rate it was taught. A week of silence costs a second.
